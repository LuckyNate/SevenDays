# SevenDays Media Notes

## Rule

Do not treat a YouTube title saying "copyright free" as sufficient permission. Before bundling any asset in the Play build, record the creator, source, license terms, required attribution, and whether redistribution inside an app/game is allowed.

## Audio candidates found

### LARA'S HORROR SOUNDS — creepy ambient horror music
Creator states that the track may be used in films and games with credit to Lara Sluyter / LARA'S HORROR SOUNDS. The description explicitly says not to redistribute the creator's content as standalone files.

Candidate source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7s-FRMb0CbY

### Myuu — Underworld
Dark ambient track. Creator states it is free to use with credit and identifies it as part of the YouTube Audio Library.

Candidate source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hZqdaWPSa5A

## Video

No creepy-video candidate has been approved for bundling yet. Search results contain many videos that use licensed music or stock footage without granting reuse rights to the complete video itself.

For the opening cursed-media sequence, prefer one of these approaches:

1. A clearly CC0/public-domain video source with documented terms.
2. Individual reusable/public-domain footage clips edited into an original SevenDays sequence.
3. Original/generated visuals plus properly licensed audio.

The goal is to make the actual cursed-phone sequence original to SevenDays rather than copying somebody else's finished horror video.

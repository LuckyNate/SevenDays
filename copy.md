# SevenDays

**You have seven days.**

SevenDays is a completely ordinary app containing a completely ordinary video followed by a completely ordinary seven-day countdown.

Nothing to worry about.

Probably.

## Features

- One complimentary seven-day countdown
- Helpful hourly reminders that time continues to pass
- Persistent countdown technology
- An exciting number that gradually gets smaller
- Convenient sharing with friends and loved ones
- Also works with acquaintances
- No complicated gameplay to learn
- Absolutely no potions to purchase
